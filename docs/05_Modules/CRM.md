# 1. CRM Module Overview

The Falcon One Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Module provides a centralized enterprise platform for managing leads, customers, contacts, opportunities, sales pipelines, communications, activities, follow-ups, and customer relationships across the entire Falcon One Business Operating System.

The CRM Module serves as the primary business relationship management platform.

Every customer interaction shall be managed through the CRM.
