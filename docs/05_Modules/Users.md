# 1. Users Module Overview

The Falcon One Users Module provides a centralized Enterprise User Management & Identity platform for managing user accounts, digital identities, authentication, authorization, profiles, preferences, sessions, devices, security, compliance, and lifecycle management across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Users Module serves as the centralized identity platform for every person and service account interacting with Falcon One.

Every identity shall be uniquely managed, securely authenticated, continuously monitored, and fully auditable throughout its lifecycle.
