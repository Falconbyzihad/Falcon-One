# 1. Sales Module Overview

The Falcon One Sales Module provides a centralized Enterprise Sales Management platform for managing quotations, opportunities, sales activities, negotiations, orders, revenue generation, and customer relationships across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Sales Module serves as the revenue generation engine connecting Leads, Customers, Orders, Payments, Shipping, and Finance.

Every sales transaction shall be traceable from the first opportunity through final payment.
