# 1. Files & Media Module Overview

The Falcon One Files & Media Module provides a centralized Enterprise Digital Asset Management (DAM) platform for storing, organizing, securing, sharing, processing, and managing files and media assets across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Files & Media Module serves as the unified repository for documents, images, videos, audio, archives, design assets, invoices, contracts, attachments, and business media.

Every digital asset shall remain secure, searchable, version-controlled, and fully traceable throughout its lifecycle.
