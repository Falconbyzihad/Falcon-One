# 1. Reports Module Overview

The Falcon One Reports Module provides a centralized Enterprise Reporting and Business Intelligence (BI) platform for collecting, processing, analyzing, visualizing, and distributing operational, financial, customer, sales, inventory, logistics, HR, CRM, and executive reports across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Reports Module serves as the centralized enterprise analytics and decision-support platform.

Every report shall be generated from validated enterprise data sources.
