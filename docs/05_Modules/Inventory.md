# 1. Inventory Module Overview

The Falcon One Inventory Module provides a centralized enterprise inventory and warehouse management platform for managing products, warehouses, stock movement, procurement, receiving, transfers, inventory valuation, replenishment, warehouse operations, and inventory intelligence across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Inventory Module serves as the centralized inventory management platform.

Every inventory transaction shall be processed through the centralized inventory engine.
