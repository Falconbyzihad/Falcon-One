# 1. HRM Module Overview

The Falcon One Human Resource Management (HRM) Module provides a centralized enterprise workforce management platform for managing employees, recruitment, onboarding, attendance, leave, payroll, performance, training, employee services, compliance, workforce analytics, and organizational operations across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The HRM Module serves as the centralized workforce management platform.

Every employee lifecycle shall be managed through the HRM Module.
