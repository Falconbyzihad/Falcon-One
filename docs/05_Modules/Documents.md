# 1. Documents Module Overview

The Falcon One Documents Module provides a centralized Enterprise Document Management System (EDMS) for creating, organizing, storing, managing, securing, approving, collaborating on, and governing business documents across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Documents Module serves as the single source of truth for contracts, invoices, quotations, purchase orders, policies, HR documents, financial documents, legal records, operational manuals, customer documents, and all enterprise business records.

Every document shall remain secure, version-controlled, permission-aware, searchable, auditable, and fully traceable throughout its lifecycle.
