# 1. Products Module Overview

The Falcon One Products Module provides a centralized Enterprise Product Information Management (PIM) platform for managing product catalogs, categories, brands, variants, pricing, inventory relationships, digital assets, product lifecycle, approvals, product intelligence, and product governance across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Products Module serves as the centralized product master data platform.

Every product shall be managed through the centralized Product Information Engine.
