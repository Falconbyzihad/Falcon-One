# 1. Shipping Module Overview

The Falcon One Shipping Module provides a centralized Enterprise Shipping and Fulfillment platform for managing shipment planning, courier operations, delivery execution, shipment tracking, proof of delivery, return logistics, and fulfillment analytics across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Shipping Module serves as the fulfillment engine connecting Orders, Payments, Inventory, Customers, Logistics, Finance, Reports, and external courier providers.

Every shipment shall remain traceable from warehouse dispatch to final customer delivery.
