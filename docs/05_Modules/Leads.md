# 1. Leads Module Overview

The Falcon One Leads Module provides a centralized Enterprise Lead Management platform for capturing, organizing, qualifying, assigning, nurturing, tracking, and converting potential customers into sales opportunities across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Leads Module serves as the entry point of the enterprise sales pipeline.

Every lead shall be traceable from its first interaction through qualification, conversion, or closure.
