# 1. AI Module Overview

The Falcon One Artificial Intelligence Module provides a centralized enterprise AI platform that delivers intelligent automation, decision support, predictive analytics, natural language processing, machine learning integration, and AI-assisted workflows across every business module within the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The AI Module acts as a shared enterprise service rather than an isolated application.

Every Falcon One module shall be capable of consuming AI services through standardized interfaces.
