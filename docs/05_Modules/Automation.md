# 1. Automation Module Overview

The Falcon One Automation Module provides a centralized enterprise automation platform that orchestrates business workflows, executes intelligent business rules, automates repetitive operations, coordinates cross-module processes, and enables event-driven automation throughout the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Automation Module serves as the operational workflow engine for every business module.

Automation shall reduce manual work while preserving business governance.
