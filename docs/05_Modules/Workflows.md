# 1. Workflows Module Overview

The Falcon One Workflows Module provides a centralized Enterprise Workflow Management platform for designing, executing, monitoring, automating, and governing business processes across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Workflows Module acts as the orchestration engine connecting all Falcon One modules through standardized business processes.

Every workflow shall be configurable, traceable, secure, and fully auditable.
