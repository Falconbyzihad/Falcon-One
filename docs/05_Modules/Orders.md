# 1. Orders Module Overview

The Falcon One Orders Module provides a centralized enterprise Order Management System (OMS) for managing quotations, draft orders, sales orders, purchase orders, fulfillment, payments, shipping, approvals, returns, customer communication, automation, and order intelligence across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Orders Module serves as the central orchestration platform for the complete order lifecycle.

Every order shall be managed through the centralized enterprise order engine.
