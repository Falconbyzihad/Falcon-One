# 1. Customers Module Overview

The Falcon One Customers Module provides a centralized enterprise customer management platform that maintains complete customer profiles, relationship history, communication records, purchase behavior, financial information, loyalty status, customer intelligence, and lifecycle management across the Falcon One Business Operating System.

The Customers Module serves as the single source of truth for all customer information.

Every business module shall reference the centralized customer repository.
